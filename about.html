"We started CELWORKS because most brand videos get watched once, out of politeness. We wanted to make the kind that get watched twice, on purpose."

The craft behind the cut.

A working animation and film studio built by artists who treat every timeline like a shot list.

Founded in 2018, CELWORKS grew out of a simple frustration: corporate videos had become generic templates. Agencies were prioritizing volume and rushing timelines, leaving films that looked polished on the surface but lacked point of view.

We took our name from the traditional animation cel, the clear acetate sheet where individual frames were drawn and painted by hand. That discipline demanded total intention: every frame took hours, so no frame was wasted.

Today, our studio brings together directors, 2D and 3D animators, motion designers, and sound engineers under one roof. We work directly with founders, creative leaders, and product teams to translate complex ideas into cinematic visual stories.

We do not pitch buzzwords or outsource your key sequences. Senior artists write the scripts, sketch the storyboards, animate the characters, and color grade every final export.

How we work.

Four operating principles that protect the craft on every production.

PRINCIPLE 01

Craft over speed

We work at the speed of good decision making. We lock scripts and storyboards early so production time is spent refining craft, not fixing foundation.

PRINCIPLE 02

Collaboration over hand-offs

You speak directly with the artists making your film. No account layers translating feedback, no creative intent lost in email chains.

PRINCIPLE 03

Curiosity over formula

Every story has an authentic rhythm. We never force a brand into a pre-baked style or recycle visual tropes from previous client reels.

PRINCIPLE 04

Precision over guesswork

From frame counts to sound mix balance and final color management, we measure twice so every export holds up on any screen.
