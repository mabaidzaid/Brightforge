## 3. Global Content Rules

- No em dashes, anywhere, in any copy.
- One consistent stats bar across every page (the reference sites showed different, inconsistent numbers on almost every page, which reads as a bug). Use this single set everywhere the stats bar appears:
  **4+ Years in Business · 8,000+ Projects Delivered · 98% Client Satisfaction · 15+ Team Members**
- One consistent set of 6 testimonials, used across every page that has a testimonials section (the reference sites repeated the same 2 to 3 testimonials with duplicated names, which also reads as a bug). Use the 6 unique testimonials in Section 5 everywhere.
- The "Our Process" 3-step section is shared and identical across the Web Development, Google Ads, and SEO service pages. Use the single version in Section 4.3 for all three.
- Voice: plain, confident, specific. Avoid vague agency language ("cutting-edge," "world-class," "unmatched"). Say what the business actually does and what the client actually gets.
- Every CTA button label should say exactly what happens next (e.g. "Order Now" leads straight to the ordering flow for that plan).
