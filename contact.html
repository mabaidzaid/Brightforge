Frequently asked questions

Standard productions range from 4 to 8 weeks depending on animation complexity and asset requirements. Whiteboard explainers average 4 weeks, while full 3D and cinematic brand films generally require 8 to 10 weeks.
We structure our workflow in sequential gates: discovery, script, storyboard, animatic, and final color/sound. Each stage includes two dedicated rounds of client review before moving forward, ensuring no unexpected surprises in post.
Upon final payment, you own full commercial rights to all delivered exports across every aspect ratio requested. Open project files and layered working assets are available on select tiers or upon studio agreement.
